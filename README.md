# Cab Booking and Driver Performance Analysis Dashboard

![Cab Booking Dashboard](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ebc8d445-e22c-4eaa-96f1-6471c317a28e)

## Overview
This project focuses on transforming raw cab booking data into an interactive Power BI dashboard to evaluate driver performance, vehicle metrics, and overall business operations.

## Situation
The cab booking data contained information about drivers, vehicle types, fares, ratings, availability, and dates. However, it was difficult to understand the overall business performance and derive meaningful insights directly from the raw data.

## Task
The objective was to analyze this dataset and create an interactive dashboard that could help stakeholders understand fare performance, vehicle-wise contribution, driver ratings, and overall vehicle availability.

## Action
Power BI was utilized to prepare the data and build an interactive report featuring KPI cards, charts, slicers, and date filters. Key analytical steps and discoveries included:

* **Core Metrics:** Processed around 1.2K driver ID records, revealing an average fare of ₹277.12 and a total fare of approximately ₹333K.
* **Vehicle Contributions:** Compared vehicle categories to find that the "Mini" segment contributed the highest total fare of ₹89K (27% of the total revenue). Meanwhile, the "Sedan" category registered the highest maximum individual fare at around ₹500.
* **Fleet Availability:** Assessed the operational status of the fleet, identifying that 52.17% of vehicles were available while 47.83% were unavailable.

## Result
The final dashboard successfully converted the raw data into an interactive visual report. This made it much easier to compare vehicle performance side-by-side, understand how different segments contribute to total fares, monitor driver ratings, and identify potential fleet availability issues. Additionally, building this project reinforced practical skills in Power BI, data visualization, KPI analysis, and generating business insights.
